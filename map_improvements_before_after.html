Finding #3 — Protocol Filter Toggle Buttons
The L3 standard shell has no protocol filter UI. Engineers cannot hide OSPF, BGP, Static, or Connected routes independently to focus on what matters during an incident.
BEFORE — No protocol filtering
All route types shown simultaneously — hairball with mixed protocols
AFTER — Protocol toggle bar
Click [BGP] to show only BGP peering edges. Click [OSPF] to see OSPF area topology. Toggle any combination.

Implementation: store edges in a map keyed by protocol. Toggle button click calls edges.update() with hidden: true/false for all edges of that protocol. Also hide nodes that become isolated (all edges hidden) with a hidden_by_filter flag. ~50 lines of JS.

Finding #5 — Combined Map: Correlation Visualization
The combined L2+L3 map renders physical and routing edges together but gives no visual indication which routing adjacency runs over which physical cable. The correlation data exists in the bundle but is not presented on canvas.

Implementation: in combined_l2l3_shell.py, when a L3 edge has a non-empty physical_link field, tag both it and its matching L2 edge with a correlation_group ID. On hover, use network.on('hoverEdge') to highlight all edges in the same group and show a correlation info box.
